Helicopter AR

Drag to turn · Pinch to zoom · Tap a button to see it move

AR isn’t available here. Open this page on a phone or iPad in Safari or Chrome to place the helicopter on your desk.

Tap a button to see what each part does.

See it on your desk

  1. Tap View on your desk.
  2. Move your device slowly over the table until the helicopter appears.
  3. Drag to move it. Pinch to make it bigger or smaller. Walk around it.

Camera mode

  1. Tap Camera and allow camera access.
  2. Point your device at your desk. Tap the buttons at the bottom to see each part move.
  3. Tap Labels to hide or show the part names, and X-ray to see inside.
  4. Tap Exit camera to return.

In AR on iPhone and iPad the model stays still, so play each movement here first.
